# CineMovie — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-08_

CineMovie ("the app") is a free movie catalogue app. This policy explains what the
app does and does not do with your data.

## Data we collect

**None.** The app has no account system, no login, no analytics SDK, no advertising
SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. We do not collect, store or share any personal or
sensitive user data, and no data leaves your device towards us — we operate no server.

## Data stored on your device

* **Favourite movies** — when you mark a movie as a favourite, the movie's public
  catalogue data (title, description, poster path, rating, release date) is saved in a
  local database inside the app's private storage.
* This data never leaves your device, except through the Android system backup that
  you control in your Google account settings.
* Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently.

## Third-party services

The app requests movie data from **TMDB (The Movie Database)** over an encrypted HTTPS
connection, and loads movie trailers from **YouTube** inside an in-app web view.
When you use those parts of the app, your request (including your IP address) reaches
those providers, and their own privacy policies apply:

* TMDB — <https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy>
* YouTube / Google — <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

## Permissions

* `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` — required to download movie data and trailers.

The app requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or identifier
permissions.

## Children

The app contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no user-generated content. Movie
descriptions and posters come from TMDB and are intended for a general audience.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL that is
listed in the app's Google Play listing.
